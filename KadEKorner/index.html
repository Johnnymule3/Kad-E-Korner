Menu

At Kad-E-Korner Diner, we serve classic American comfort food with a homemade touch.
Try our juicy burgers, crispy fried chicken, and savory meatloaf smothered in gravy.
Start your day right with fluffy pancakes, sizzling bacon, and biscuits with sausage gravy.
Save room for our homemade pies and creamy milkshakes - the perfect sweet finish.
Every dish is made with fresh, local ingredients and served with small-town charm.

Events

At Kad-E-Korner Diner, we serve up great food and live entertainment.
Every weekend, enjoy local bands playing everything from blues to acoustic sets while you dine on our homestyle favorites.
Come for the food, stay for the music, and join the fun at your local diner.
